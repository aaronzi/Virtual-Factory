X-PRODUCTION-DAY - iCalendar extension property (RFC 5545, section 3.8.8.2 "Non-Standard Properties")
Production calendar of LINE01, VF Pneumatics GmbH, Plant 01 (IDTA 02067 Production Calendar)

Purpose:     Assigns a VEVENT (a production time slot, i.e. a shift) to a production day.
Value type:  INTEGER; one of 1, 0, -1.
Used in:     Every VEVENT of the calendar (shifts "Early shift" 06:00-14:00, "Late shift" 14:00-22:00, Mon-Fri,
             and the monthly maintenance window).
Example:     X-PRODUCTION-DAY:0

Interpretation:
- The value refers to the end (DTEND) of the event:
    1  the event belongs to the following production day,
    0  the event belongs to the production day of its calendar day,
   -1  the event belongs to the previous production day.
- All shifts of LINE01 end on the day they start, therefore the value is 0.
- Times are local time Europe/Berlin (VTIMEZONE in the calendar file).
- EXDATE entries remove public holidays (Rhineland-Palatinate) from the recurring shifts.
