X-PRODUCTION-DAY - iCalendar extension property (RFC 5545, section 3.8.8.2 "Non-Standard Properties")
Production calendar of LINE01, VF Pneumatics GmbH, Plant 01 (IDTA 02067 Production Calendar)

Purpose:     Marks a VEVENT as a production time slot in which the asset is planned to produce.
Value type:  BOOLEAN (TRUE / FALSE); default FALSE if the property is absent.
Used in:     VEVENT (recurring shift events, e.g. "Early shift" 06:00-14:00, "Late shift" 14:00-22:00, Mon-Fri).
Example:     X-PRODUCTION-DAY:TRUE

Interpretation:
- The union of all VEVENT occurrences with X-PRODUCTION-DAY:TRUE is the planned operation time of the asset.
- Occurrences with X-BREAK:TRUE or X-MAINTENANCE:TRUE that overlap a production slot are subtracted from it
  (planned down time); the remainder is the planned busy time (ISO 22400-2, PBT).
- EXDATE entries remove public holidays (Rhineland-Palatinate) from the recurring shifts.
- Times are local time Europe/Berlin (VTIMEZONE in the calendar file).
- CATEGORIES:PRODUCTION is set in addition for clients that ignore non-standard properties.
