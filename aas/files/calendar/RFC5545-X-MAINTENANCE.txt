X-MAINTENANCE - iCalendar extension property (RFC 5545, section 3.8.8.2 "Non-Standard Properties")
Production calendar of LINE01, VF Pneumatics GmbH, Plant 01 (IDTA 02067 Production Calendar)

Purpose:     Lists the periods inside a VEVENT that are reserved for planned maintenance or repair of the asset.
Value type:  Comma separated list of PERIOD values (RFC 5545 section 3.3.9): <start>/<end> or <start>/<duration>.
Used in:     The monthly preventive maintenance window (first Saturday of the month 06:00-10:00).
Example:     X-MAINTENANCE:20251004T040000Z/PT4H

Interpretation:
- The period is given for the first occurrence of the event. For recurring events it applies to every occurrence
  at the same offset from the event start.
- The asset is not available for production during a maintenance period (planned down time, ISO 22400-2).
- The maintenance content is defined in the MaintenanceInstructions submodels of the line's assets
  (e.g. RB01/UR5e: MI-UR5E-01 monthly safety check).
