X-MAINTENANCE - iCalendar extension property (RFC 5545, section 3.8.8.2 "Non-Standard Properties")
Production calendar of LINE01, VF Pneumatics GmbH, Plant 01 (IDTA 02067 Production Calendar)

Purpose:     Marks a VEVENT as a time slot reserved for planned maintenance or repair of the asset.
Value type:  BOOLEAN (TRUE / FALSE); default FALSE if the property is absent.
Used in:     VEVENT (monthly preventive maintenance, first Saturday of the month 06:00-10:00).
Example:     X-MAINTENANCE:TRUE

Interpretation:
- The asset is not available for production during a maintenance slot; overlapping production slots are reduced
  accordingly (planned down time, ISO 22400-2).
- The maintenance content is defined in the MaintenanceInstructions submodels of the line's assets
  (e.g. RB01/UR5e: MI-UR5E-01 monthly safety check).
- CATEGORIES:MAINTENANCE is set in addition for clients that ignore non-standard properties.
