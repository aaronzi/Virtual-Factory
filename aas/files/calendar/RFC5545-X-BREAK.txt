X-BREAK - iCalendar extension property (RFC 5545, section 3.8.8.2 "Non-Standard Properties")
Production calendar of LINE01, VF Pneumatics GmbH, Plant 01 (IDTA 02067 Production Calendar)

Purpose:     Lists the break periods inside a VEVENT in which the asset is not planned to produce.
Value type:  Comma separated list of PERIOD values (RFC 5545 section 3.3.9): <start>/<end> or <start>/<duration>.
Used in:     The shift events (collective breaks 10:00-10:30 in the early shift and 18:00-18:30 in the late shift).
Example:     X-BREAK:20250915T080000Z/PT30M

Interpretation:
- The period is given for the first occurrence of the event. For recurring events it applies to every occurrence
  at the same offset from the event start (the break starts 4 hours after the start of the early shift).
- A break is subtracted from the shift it lies in (planned down time, ISO 22400-2); the remainder is the planned
  busy time.
- During a break the line is brought into the PackML state Held by the MES and resumed (Unhold) afterwards;
  the break time counts as planned down time, not as unit down time.
